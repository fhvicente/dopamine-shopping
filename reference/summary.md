**Website:** dopamineshopping.com – a German‑language, parody e‑commerce portal that markets itself as “the only honest shop on the Internet” and promises a shopping‑induced dopamine rush with a final price of 0,00 €.
https://dopamineshopping.com/

**Core purpose & overall intent**  
The site is a comedic “free‑shopping” experience: visitors can “buy” high‑end tech, beauty or home items, but no money is taken, no personal or card data is stored, and no real product is shipped. The aim is to generate the thrill of shopping without any financial or legal obligations.

**Key marketing messages**  
- “Delivery by (almost) real drivers” – live‑tracking shows packages being swallowed by whales, abducted by UFOs, etc.  
- “One‑click auto‑payment that pays itself” (the invoice never arrives).  
- “100 % fake, 200 % dopamine” and “the final price is always 0,00 €”  
- “Premium welcome offer – 25 % off the first order” (coupon shown).  
- “Blitzangebote” (flash deals) with absurd discounts up to 38 %.

**Main advertised features**  
- Free or heavily discounted (often non‑existent) products.  
- Live‑tracking icon and animated delivery map.  
- Auto‑payment mock‑process that “pays itself.”  
- Optional account creation that adds gamification (XP, achievements, leaderboard).  
- Availability in 12 languages with local currency displays.  
- Donation option to have a real product added to the catalog.

**Navigation & categories**  
Shop navigation includes: Games, Tech, Beauty, Fashion, Home. Supporting links: About, Suggestions, Support, Order tracking, Returns, Donation, and humorous “Payment” options (PayPal, imaginary card, never‑arriving invoice).

**Product highlights (sample listings)**  
- High‑end graphics cards: Nvidia GeForce RTX 4090 24 GB, RTX 4080 variants (PNY, MSI, Zotac, Asus ROG Strix) with original and “‑16 %” discounted prices.  
- Gaming/Workstation PCs: configurations with Intel i9 or AMD Ryzen 9 7900X3D CPUs, shown with installment plans.  
- Beauty and home items: LED lamps, facial creams, masks, also listed with absurd discounts.

**FAQ highlights**  
- The shop is not a real retailer; all payments and deliveries are fictitious.  
- No personal or card data is retained.  
- The service is completely free and safe.  

**Overall impression**  
Dopamineshopping.com is a satire‑styled storefront that uses exaggerated promises, fake discounts and the concept of “free dopamine” to attract clicks. It does not sell real goods; instead it offers a humorous, dopamine‑inducing shopping simulation.
